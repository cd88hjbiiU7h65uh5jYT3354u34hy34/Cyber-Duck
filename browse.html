How To Connect Your Own Bot

Go To Bot's Website

First, go to your bot’s website and enter your phone number in the format `947XXXXXXXX`. Then click the Generate Code button. After that, copy the generated code.

Go To WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp and click the three-dot menu icon in the top-right corner. From the menu that appears, click Linked devices.

Link the Bot

Next, click the green “Link a device” button. On the screen that appears for scanning a QR code, click “Link with phone number instead” at the bottom. Then paste the code you copied earlier.
